## Pencil
- Handwrite: any text area 都可以通过pencil进行输入
- Delete: Remove editable text just by scratching it out. 横向或竖向来回涂抹，超过text
- Select: Circle or draw a line through text. 横线只要在text的范围内都可以（上、中、下）
- Insert: Tap and hold with your pencil in any editable field to make room to write additional text. 🖊点到单词之间的空隙，按住不动，等 `room` 出现之后再在 `room` 区域新添加新的文本
- Join: drawing a vertical line
