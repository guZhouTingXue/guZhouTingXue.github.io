## 名词性从句中的应用
### 主语从句
完整的形式
It + be/seem + () adjective/past participle/noun + that + subj. + (should) + v.

It 作为形式主语，后面的that是真正的主语

- It has been decided that the meeting should be postponed till next Saturday.

### 宾语从句
subj. + () verb + that + subj. + (should) + v.
额，后面的subj 是从句的主语，EG中还是当作object

- He asked that he be given an opportunity to explain his case.
