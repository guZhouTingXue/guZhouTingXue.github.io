6. 大于1时基本单位不需要变为复数：two hundred 
7. 基数词的复数 + of 表示不确定数目：
- hundreds of: 大几百，好几百
- thousands of: 大几千

## Fractions
numerator/denominator

3种表示方法：
1. a(one)half:1/2
numerator: cardinal numerals
denominator: ordinal numerals 

- 1/3: a(one) third
- 1/4: a(one) quarter/a (one) fourth
- 2/3: two thirds

2. one over/by five:1/5
numerator, denominator: cardinal numerals
- 1/100: one over a hundred/one by one hundred

3. one divided by ten:1/10
numerator, denominator: cardinal numerals
- 25/100: twenty-five divided by one hundred
