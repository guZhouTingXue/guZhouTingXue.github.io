## Mindmap
>It comes from the Latin word `perfectum`, which means "complete."

### Present Perfect
**I have seen `Star Wars`.**
three pieces of information:
- the event is finished: 
- the exact time of the action is unknown or unimportant.
- the experience of seeing `Star Wars` has some effect in the present

the difference between the simple past and present perfect:
when there is a specific time:
- "I saw `Star Wars` last night."
unknown or unimportant:
- "I have seen `Star Wars` before."
- "I have seen `Star Wars` in the past."
- "I have seen `Star Wars` three times."

### Past Perfect
>The past perfect describes an activity that was finished before another event in the past.

1. first action: had followed by a past participle verb
2. second action: use before or by the time followed by the simple past verb
关于 first action 和 second action
这里first action 对应的是先完成的动作，而不是sentence中先出现的
如果先完成的action在前面说明，那么就要用 before or by the time + second action
如果先完成的在后面，before 放在句子后面
<span style="background:#fff88f">问：</span>before 总是跟着 first action？

- She <span style="background:#ff4d4f">had already had</span> a baby <span style="background:#9254de">before</span> she <span style="background:#40a9ff">graduated</span>.
- The soldier <span style="background:#40a9ff">wasn't</span> scared because he <span style="background:#ff4d4f">had already been</span> in battle <span style="background:#9254de">before</span>.

### Future Perfect 05:33
>Use the future perfect when you know one future action will be completed before another future action.
