### past perfect progressive
>The past perfect progressive emphasizes the duration of a past action before another action happened.

- I had been smoking for 10 years before I quit.

四个组成部分：
1. had been followed by an `-ing` verb.
2. adverbs for and since: to express duration
3. adverbs before, when or by the time: introduce a second action
4. second action: uses the simple past tense.

more example:
- I <span style="background:#ff4d4f">had been studying</span> <span style="background:#fff88f">for 12 years</span> <span style="background:#40a9ff">by the time</span> <span style="background:#affad1">I graduated</span> from high school.
- She <span style="background:#ff4d4f">had been living</span> there <span style="background:#fff88f">since</span> <span style="background:#affad1">she was</span> a child.
- He <span style="background:#ff4d4f">had been teaching</span> <span style="background:#fff88f">for 12 years</span> <span style="background:#40a9ff">before</span> <span style="background:#affad1">he was</span> certified.

<span style="background:#fff88f">问：</span>如果second action 是 was 那express duration的只能是before？或者没有这个adver？

**describe a recently completed action**
- My clothes were wet because it had been raining.
- He was talking loudly because he had been drinking.

这里recently 的应该是second action

### Future perfect progressive 05:20
>The future perfect progressive describes the duration of an action as it relates to a future event.

two form:
1. will have been plus a present participle, followed by "when" or "by the time" and the second action
- I will have been working 35 years by the time I retire
- The doctor will have been working for 24 hours by the time his shift is finished.
the second planned action, retire, is in the simple present
<span style="background:#fff88f">问：</span>为什么发生在future的second action(future event)的tense 要用simple present？因为first action 已经表明了它是future？它只能是future？所以这个时态就要用最简单的-simple present


2. be going to have been plus a present participle followed by "when" or "by the time" and the second action
- By the time the plane arrives, I am going to have been waiting for five hours. 

时态只关注duration-到second action 发生的间隔时长，对于first action 是已经开始 还是没有开始 是不清楚的。
- 医生是否已经工作不清楚
- 是否正在候机不清楚，可能当前是早上8点，飞机下午7点到，我会在下午2点到机场等-动作未发生。也可能是现在下午3点，已经等了1个小时。
