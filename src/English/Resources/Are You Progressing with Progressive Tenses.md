### Present Progressive
- She is starting school next semester.
- She is going to start school next semester.

My neighbor is always playing loud music at night.
<span style="background:#fff88f">问：</span>这里和 使用simple present 有什么区别？
> You can also use present progressive with always to say that something disturbs you.

使用 present progressive 可以表达情绪，而simple present 只是陈述事实，无法体现主管感受？


### Past Progressive
"I was working late last night." - 我昨晚有加班？

>often have two actions

几种类型：
1. 没有second action，有时间
2. 有second action：express an action in progress interrupted by a second action.
在second action 之前，first action 已经发生并持续一段时间，在second action处结束
- "I was sleeping when you called"
第二个动词simple past

3. 有second action:
"It was snowing when the plane landed in Denver."
这句话的含义是什么？飞机降落时Denver正在下雪？
如何表述：
- 飞机降落的同时开始下雪：天空开始飘雪花
- 飞机降落的时候雪已经下了一段时间：地面被雪覆盖

4. 有second acton，且 two actions are happening at the same time 
- I was sleeping when you were working.
second action 使用past progressive

### Future Progressive
I will be waiting for you when you finish work
问：这里when you finish work，you 在有没有work？


- I am going to be studying later：有学习计划
- I will be studying later: 强调学习会持续一段时间？
