## Mindmap
``` markmap
# May Might Must Modals
## Degrees of certainty in the presetn
 - 接不定式一般式
   - 100%: I am sick.
   - 95%: I must be sick
   - 50%: I may be sick; I might be sick; I could be sick.
     
## Might as the past tense of may
 - Modals change to a past form in reported speech
   - I may take more pictures (direct quote)
   - She said she might take more pictures (reported speech)
 - May changes to might to express a possible state in the past
   - She might have stayed home
## Degrees of uncertainty in the future
 - You might not do well on the test
   
## May is sometimes used to express hope
	- May it be an evening star.
```

>People today do not always follow these rules about present and past tense for may and might.

- might 比 may 更委婉
